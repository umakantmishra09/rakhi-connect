# Rakhi Connect ❤️

A virtual Raksha Bandhan experience designed for brothers and sisters who are far away from each other.

**Rakhi Connect** allows siblings to share a digital Raksha Bandhan moment from anywhere. The sister can choose a Rakhi and tie it virtually, while the brother experiences the moment from his own device.

## ✨ Features

* 🎀 Interactive virtual Rakhi tying experience
* ❤️ Designed for siblings living far away from each other
* 🎊 Celebration experience after tying Rakhi
* 🎁 Send a Rakhi gift to your sister
* 📱 Fully responsive and mobile-friendly
* 🔊 Interactive sound effects
* ☕ Buy the developer a coffee
* 🌐 Simple static website

## 📱 How It Works

1. Open the website.
2. The sister starts the Rakhi experience.
3. Choose a Rakhi.
4. Tie the Rakhi virtually.
5. Celebrate the moment together.
6. Optionally send a Rakhi gift.

## 💡 Why Rakhi Connect?

Raksha Bandhan is about the bond between a brother and sister, not physical distance.

Rakhi Connect was created to make it possible for siblings living in different cities, states, or countries to still share a small Raksha Bandhan moment together.

> Distance changes the place, not the bond. ❤️
